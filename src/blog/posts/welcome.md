Most mood journals fail for a simple reason: they ask you to start from nothing on the exact days when you have the least energy to spare.

I built **NeuroNook** (`NeuroNook: Mood Journal` on the App Store) to make a daily check-in feel light, concrete, and genuinely private on iPhone and iPad.

## Play through your check-in instead of staring at a blank box

In NeuroNook, a daily check-in is nine short, construction-themed mini-games covering six areas of wellbeing: **sleep, stress, mood, motivation, social connection, and energy**. You tap bricks, connect pipes, level a foundation, plug in lights, and place roof tiles to answer how your day felt.

When you finish, your answers add an illustrated house to your seven-day neighborhood street. You can add a short journal note afterward—or skip writing completely and let the house stand for today.

## Built for real life, not perfect streaks

- **ADHD-friendly by design:** Replacing empty text boxes with quick tactile prompts lowers the friction of getting started, especially on tired or distracted evenings. Learn more on the [NeuroNook for ADHD](/adhd/) page.
- **No accounts, ads, or tracking:** Your check-ins, scores, and notes stay on your device, protected by iOS encryption. Read the full [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details on optional Live Weather and NeuroNook Plus.
- **Yours to revisit and export:** Browse past days in Calendar and Trends, customize your neighborhood look, or export your entries as JSON or PDF whenever you want.
