Finding clear, practical, and neurodiversity-affirming information online can take a lot of sifting. Whether you are newly exploring ADHD, autism, or AuDHD—or just looking for everyday strategies that respect how your brain works—these creators, podcasts, and resources are a solid place to start.

## YouTube channels for ADHD and autism

- **How to ADHD (Jessica McCabe):** One of the most approachable starting points on YouTube, with research-informed videos on executive dysfunction, time blindness, emotional regulation, masking, and practical routines.
- **ADHD Love (Rich & Rox Pink):** Honest, relatable short-form and long-form videos (plus the *LATE BLOOMERS* podcast) exploring late-diagnosed ADHD and autism, relationships, and everyday household friction without shame.
- **Yo Samdy Sam (Samantha Stein):** Thoughtful deep dives into adult autism, AuDHD (having both ADHD and autism), masking, burnout, and navigating assessments.
- **Purple Ella:** Warm, practical videos from a late-diagnosed autistic and ADHD coach covering sensory processing, shutdowns, meltdowns, and self-understanding.

## Podcasts worth queueing up

- **Hacking Your ADHD (Will Curb):** Short, focused episodes packed with actionable experiments for motivation, task initiation, sleep, and organizing around an ADHD brain.
- **The Neurodivergent Woman (Monique Mitchelson & Dr. Michelle Livock):** Hosted by a clinical psychologist and clinical neuropsychologist, covering ADHD, autism, masking, late diagnosis, and neurodivergent wellbeing with clarity and care.
- **ADHD Experts Podcast (ADDitude):** Long-running webinar and interview series featuring clinicians and researchers on topics like emotional dysregulation, rejection sensitivity, and daily habits.
