NeuroNook (listed as NeuroNook: Mood Journal on the App Store) is a daily check-in journal for iPhone and iPad. You answer nine questions by playing short construction mini-games, and each finished check-in adds a house to your street.

I built it because many journals open on an empty page, and an empty page is hard to face at the end of a tired day. NeuroNook starts with a question and something to tap.

## What a daily check-in looks like

Each day has nine questions across six areas: sleep, stress, mood, motivation, social connection and energy. A question might read "How was your sleep quality last night?" You answer by tapping a brick to fill a gap in a wall.

Other questions use other games. You level a foundation, fit pipes together, lay roof tiles or flip a light switch. Each question takes a few seconds, and you can skip one you would rather not answer.

After the ninth question you can add a short note. The note is optional. When you save, NeuroNook gives you a wellness score for the day and a house for your street.

## Your neighborhood

The Home screen shows your last seven days as a row of houses. Each house reflects that day's answers. The Calendar keeps the older days, one house per day, grouped by month on named streets.

You can look back at a day, read the note you left, and export your journal whenever you choose.

## What is free and what is Plus

The core app is free. It includes the daily check-in with 10 mini-games, optional notes, Calendar history, exports, the Today's House widget, Lock Screen widgets, reminders you set yourself, and the reading guides in the Learning Center.

NeuroNook Plus is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly. It adds 14 more mini-games, Live Weather on your street, the Street Strip widget with your last seven days, Trends charts of your check-ins, a daily backup to your own private iCloud, every hat and face for your avatar, and up to three questions of your own. The app shows the price and any free trial before you subscribe.

## Privacy

You do not need an account. Your journal is stored on your device, and NeuroNook has no ads. Two Plus features reach outside the device, and both are optional. Live Weather sends a rounded location to Apple to fetch the weather. iCloud backup copies your journal to your own private iCloud. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains both.

## The other Neuro.Nook

Two apps have similar names. NeuroNook: Mood Journal is the app described here. [Neuro.Nook](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/neuro-nook/id6754170267) is a brain-training game app from Epic Genius LLC. The two are separate apps from separate developers.

## Who it is for

I made NeuroNook first for neurodiverse people, and for anyone who has tried a mood journal and quit. It works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, in English and Spanish, and follows your system language.

NeuroNook is for personal reflection and general wellness. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat anything.
