# NeuroNook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

NeuroNook is a private mental-wellness journal. This policy describes exactly what
the app does with your information - which is, almost entirely, nothing beyond
keeping it on your device.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you record in NeuroNook - daily check-in answers, wellness scores,
journal notes, and custom questions - is stored only on your device, protected by
iOS file encryption tied to your device passcode.

NeuroNook has **no accounts, no ads, and no tracking**. Nothing you enter is ever
transmitted off your device by the app. The only other times the app goes online
are the optional Live Weather feature and the service that manages optional
Plus purchases, both explained below.

## What the app stores (on device only)

- Daily check-in answers and wellness scores
- Journal notes and custom questions
- Notification preferences and reminder history
- Appearance and accessibility settings

## Location

If you allow it, NeuroNook uses your approximate location on your device to match
the neighborhood sky to your local sunrise and sunset. NeuroNook does not store
your location, and the app works fully without it.

Live Weather is optional and stays off until you turn it on. When it is on,
NeuroNook sends your location, rounded to about 11 km (7 mi), to Apple Weather
(Apple's WeatherKit service) each time it checks the weather. That happens about
every 30 minutes while the app is open, and it is the only time your location
leaves your device. As with any internet request, Apple's servers also see your
network address. Your check-ins, journal notes, scores, and settings are never
part of the request. NeuroNook does not save the location it sends, and it keeps
only the latest weather reading on your device.

Apple states that it uses location only to provide weather forecasts, does not
associate it with personally identifiable information, and does not track it
between requests. Turning Live Weather off in Settings → Appearance → Weather
stops the requests and deletes the saved reading. Weather data comes from Apple
Weather, and the app links to Apple's page listing its other data sources.

## Notifications

Reminders and supportive notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on
your device. No notification content or behavior is sent anywhere.

## Purchases

NeuroNook Plus is an optional purchase sold through the App Store: a
subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock. Apple
processes the payment with your Apple Account, so NeuroNook never receives your
name, email address, or payment details.

To manage purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that confirms
purchases with Apple and tells the app whether Plus is on. The app contacts
RevenueCat when it opens and when you view the Plus offer, buy Plus, or restore a
purchase. RevenueCat sees a random ID it creates for this install, your NeuroNook
purchase history (which plan, when, and whether it renews), your device's vendor
ID and model, your iOS and app versions, your language and App Store country and,
as with any internet request, your IP address. It uses these to confirm purchases
and to count purchases and trials. Your check-ins, journal notes, and scores are
never part of that. RevenueCat is not used for ads or tracking, and its handling
of data is covered by its own privacy policy. Apple's handling of purchases is
covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Sharing you control

Exporting your journal or emailing feedback happens only when you choose it,
using the standard iOS share and mail interfaces. What you share, and with whom,
is entirely up to you.

## Deleting your data

You can erase everything stored on your device at any time in the app under
**Settings → Data Management**. Deleting the app does the same. RevenueCat keeps
its own anonymous purchase record, which neither action removes.

## Children

NeuroNook is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone - including children - because it has no accounts and no
servers of its own. The optional Live Weather feature is the one place the app
contacts a service, and it is described under Location.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of NeuroNook ever changes this posture (for example, by
adding optional sync), this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will
be updated first, and any new data use will be opt-in.
