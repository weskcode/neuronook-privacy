# About NeuroNook & Wesley Keetch

**NeuroNook** (`NeuroNook: Mood Journal` on the Apple App Store) is a private, local-first wellness journal for iPhone and iPad created by **Wesley Keetch** (`@weskcode`).

## Why NeuroNook exists

Traditional mood trackers and journaling apps often fail right when you need them most—on tired, distracted, or overwhelmed days. Staring at a blank page can feel like homework, and many popular wellness apps ask for accounts, send telemetry, or punish you for missing a day.

NeuroNook replaces the blank page with nine short, construction-themed mini-games covering sleep, stress, mood, motivation, social connection, and energy. Finishing a check-in builds a house on a seven-day neighborhood street, turning daily reflection into something tangible and low-pressure while keeping every entry on your device.

## About Wesley Keetch (`weskcode`)

**Wesley Keetch** (also known online as **Wes Keetch** or **`weskcode`**) is an independent iOS, macOS, and desktop software developer based in New York City. Alongside **NeuroNook**, his indie and open-source projects focus on calm, privacy-respecting tools and ADHD-friendly workflows—including **Nudge** (a full-screen hyperfocus break reminder app), **CheatSheet** (a native Swift developer notes app), **RHOIDS** (a privacy-first toilet health timer), **SunHat**, and **Words of Winter**.
