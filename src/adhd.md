# NeuroNook for ADHD & Neurodivergent Minds

Most journaling and habit apps break down for the exact same reason: they hand you a blank page or a twenty-field form and expect steady executive function every night.

**NeuroNook** takes a different path. Created by independent iOS developer [Wesley Keetch](/about/), it turns a daily wellness check-in into nine short, tactile construction mini-games on iPhone and iPad—so you can reflect on your day even when words or energy feel scarce.

## Why blank-page apps are hard with ADHD

- **Initiation friction:** Staring at an empty text box triggers task paralysis. Tapping a brick, sliding a pipe, or placing a roof tile gives your hands an immediate, concrete starting point.
- **Difficulty naming feelings:** When someone asks *"How are you feeling today?"*, the answer is rarely one neat word. Splitting a check-in into six areas—sleep, stress, mood, motivation, social connection, and energy—makes reflection bite-sized.
- **All-or-nothing streak guilt:** Missing two days in a rigid tracker often makes people abandon the whole app. NeuroNook’s seven-day neighborhood simply builds a house for the days you check in, and even a tough day still gets a house.
- **Notification noise and clutter:** NeuroNook supports system **Reduce Motion**, Dynamic Type, English and Spanish, and quiet, user-chosen reminders without social feeds, ads, or pop-ups.

## What a daily check-in looks like

1. **Play nine quick construction games:** Each question takes seconds. Answer by interacting—no essays required.
2. **Watch your neighborhood grow:** Every completed check-in places a house onto your illustrated seven-day street, reflecting your day.
3. **Add a note only if you want one:** Written reflection is always optional. A house and a few words—or no words at all—is enough.
4. **Look back at your own pace:** Explore past check-ins in Calendar, spot patterns in Trends, read built-in **Learning Center** guides (including *ADHD Support* and *Autism Awareness* topics), or export your entries as JSON or PDF.

## Private by default, built by an indie developer

Mental health and wellness entries are deeply personal. NeuroNook stores your check-ins, scores, and notes **only on your device**—with no accounts, no ads, and no analytics SDKs of its own. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for full details on optional Live Weather and NeuroNook Plus.

- **Free core app:** Daily check-ins, journal reflections, Calendar history, exports, Today’s House and Lock Screen widgets, and reminders stay free.
- **Optional Plus:** Adds Live Weather on your street, the full-week Street Strip widget, all avatar hats and faces, and up to three custom questions.

*Note: NeuroNook is a personal wellness and self-reflection journal, not a medical device, and does not provide diagnosis, therapy, or clinical treatment.*
