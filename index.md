# Privacy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

NeuroNook has no accounts, ads or tracking. Your check-ins and journal stay on your device; you choose any sharing.

## On your device

Your check-in answers, wellness scores, journal notes and custom questions are protected by iOS file encryption tied to your passcode. Reminder preferences/history and appearance/accessibility settings also stay on your device. Reminders and supportive notifications are scheduled locally; their content and behavior are not sent anywhere.

Journal notes, check-in answers and scores are not sent to these services. Weather requests also exclude settings. Apple and RevenueCat handle their data under their own privacy policies.

## Weather controls

Location permission is optional. The app works without it; with permission, approximate location sets sunrise/sunset locally. NeuroNook does not store location. Live Weather is the only time location leaves the device, and only the latest weather reading is kept.

Turning off **Settings → Appearance → Weather** stops requests and deletes that reading. Apple states that forecast location is not associated with personally identifiable information or tracked between requests, and is used only for forecasts. The app links to Apple Weather’s other data sources.

## Sharing and deletion

Exports and emailed feedback use standard iOS share/mail interfaces only when you choose what to share and with whom. Erase local data in **Settings → Data Management**, or delete the app. Neither action removes RevenueCat’s anonymous purchase record.

## Children and policy changes

NeuroNook is not directed at children under 13. The weather and purchase services above do not receive your journal entries, answers or scores.

Before introducing new data uses, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details; new data uses will be opt-in.
