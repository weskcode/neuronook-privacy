# Privacy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

NeuroNook has no accounts, ads or tracking. Your check-ins and journal stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud Backup, which copies them to your own private iCloud. You choose any sharing.

## On your device

Your check-in answers, wellness scores, journal notes and custom questions are protected by iOS file encryption tied to your passcode. Reminder preferences/history and appearance/accessibility settings also stay on your device. Reminders and supportive notifications are scheduled locally; their content and behavior are not sent anywhere. If you turn on iCloud Backup, a copy of this data also goes to your own private iCloud (see below).

Journal notes, check-in answers and scores are never sent to Apple Weather or RevenueCat. With iCloud Backup on, they are copied only to your own private iCloud. Weather requests also exclude settings. Apple and RevenueCat handle their data under their own privacy policies.

## Weather controls

Location permission is optional. The app works without it; with permission, approximate location sets sunrise/sunset locally. NeuroNook does not store location. Live Weather, part of NeuroNook Plus, is the only time location leaves the device, and only the latest weather reading is kept.

Turning off Live Weather in **Settings → Weather** stops requests and deletes that reading. Apple states that forecast location is not associated with personally identifiable information or tracked between requests, and is used only for forecasts. The app links to Apple Weather’s other data sources.

## Points and decorations (when enabled)

When points are enabled, NeuroNook keeps reward records in its on-device store. These include the enrollment day and time zone, recorded times, credited calendar days, points, unlocked decorations and the decorations you choose. Pending completion records also keep an internal check-in ID. Your answers, wellness scores and notes are not copied into reward records.

Deleting one journal entry keeps its dated reward record and any earned points. A saved completion still waiting for points can earn later even if you delete its journal entry. Journal exports do not include reward records. Delete all data removes reward records, unlocks and selections. NeuroNook does not send reward records to us. Your iCloud Backup, if you turn it on, and backups managed by your device may include them.

## Sharing and deletion

Exports and emailed feedback use standard iOS share/mail interfaces only when you choose what to share and with whom. Erase local data in **Settings → Data Management**, or delete the app. Delete all data keeps three iCloud Backup details on this device: the dates of backups, which iCloud account was used, and a note that data was deleted. Neither action deletes purchase records held by Apple or, when used, RevenueCat, or your iCloud Backup. Delete the backup with **Delete iCloud Backup** in the same screen, or in your device's iCloud storage settings.

If NeuroNook Plus ends, backups stop and your last backup stays in iCloud, so you can still restore it. Restoring needs a device signed in to the same Apple Account. Automatic backups pause if your device signs in to a different iCloud account.

## Children and policy changes

NeuroNook is not directed at children under 13. The weather and purchase services above do not receive your journal entries, answers or scores.

Before introducing new data uses, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details; new data uses will be opt-in.
