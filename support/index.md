# Support

Email [support@neuronook.app](mailto:support@neuronook.app) for help, bugs or privacy questions.

- **Include:** your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the problem.
- **Leave out:** journal entries and personal notes. Support cannot see them; they stay on your device.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for how the app handles your information.
