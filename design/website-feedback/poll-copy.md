# Copy for online or in-person feedback

## Introduction

I'm choosing a website design for NeuroNook, a wellness journal for iPhone and iPad. Please look at all three concepts before answering. There are no right answers. Your feedback will help choose a direction before the website changes.

## Questions

1. Which website would make you most interested in trying NeuroNook? A / B / C / None / Tie (name the options).
2. What made you choose it?
3. Based on the images, what do you think the app does?
4. Was anything unclear or hard to read? Please name the option.
5. Have you used or seen NeuroNook before? Yes / No / Not sure.

Please avoid sharing personal wellness information in your answer.

## Short social post

Which website would make you most interested in trying NeuroNook, a wellness journal for iPhone and iPad?

Look at A, B, and C, then reply with your choice and a short reason. None or a tie is fine. What do you think the app does, and was anything hard to read?

These are draft concepts. The live website has not changed.

## In-person facilitator

Show the introduction, then each concept at the same size. Let the participant finish looking before advancing. Show the comparison last. Ask the questions without offering your own preference. Record the answer privately before inviting discussion.
