# NeuroNook support

Email [weskcode@duck.com](mailto:weskcode@duck.com) if you need help or want to report a bug. If something is not working, include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Please do not send journal entries or other personal notes by email. They stay on your device, and support cannot see them.

Privacy questions go to the same address. The [privacy policy](../) covers what the app does with your information.
